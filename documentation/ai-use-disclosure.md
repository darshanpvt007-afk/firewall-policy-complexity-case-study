## Statement for the report (place after the Individual Contribution Statement)

**Use of AI tools.** We used an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) during this project for the following tasks:

- proposing an initial structure for the report and the experiment;
- drafting early versions of text, which team members then checked against the sources and revised;
- writing first versions of the Packet Tracer configuration scripts, the test matrix and a script that counts ACL entries and rule-order relations from configuration text;
- checking the documents for internal consistency (for example, that planned counts matched the configuration scripts);
- checking bibliographic details of references against online listings.

The team was responsible for:

- choosing and approving the research question, topology, policy stages and test subset (recorded in our decisions log);
- reading the cited sources and confirming each claim we attribute to them [TEAM CHECK: true only once Appendix B is complete];
- building the network in Cisco Packet Tracer, running every test and capturing every screenshot and result;
- the interpretation of results and the final wording of the report.

No experimental result, screenshot or reference in this report was produced by the AI tool. All results come from our own Packet Tracer runs.
